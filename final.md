1. 🎮 Improve the Core Gameplay
A. Make the 5-Lane Battlefield More Strategic
Currently, players summon defenders and wait for enemies to approach.

That works, but the game needs more meaningful decisions.

I recommend introducing three battlefield mechanics.

1. Corrupted Tiles

Certain battlefield tiles become corrupted during enemy waves.

Corrupted tiles could:

Reduce defender attack speed.
Slowly damage defenders.
Increase enemy movement speed.
Become purified when specific guardians are deployed.
This encourages players to think carefully about placement.

2. Soul Crystal Abilities

The Soul Crystal should actively participate in combat.

Give it three unlockable abilities:

Ability	Effect
Soul Pulse	Pushes nearby enemies backward
Crystal Storm	Damages enemies across all lanes
Divine Barrier	Temporarily protects the crystal
These abilities should have cooldowns.

3. Emergency Defense Mechanic

When enemies approach the bottom of a lane, the player can activate an emergency defense.

For example:

Last Stand: Once per battle, the Soul Crystal unleashes a powerful blast that destroys weaker enemies near its position and damages stronger enemies.

This creates exciting comeback moments.

Apple Compliance
Digital goods sold inside an iOS app generally must use Apple's In-App Purchase system unless a specific exception applies.

If you integrate advertising, ensure appropriate privacy disclosures and consent handling.

Do not require players to watch advertisements to complete campaign stages.

For an initial launch, the simplest option is a completely playable free game with no monetization, followed by a monetization update once the core gameplay is validated.

12. 🍎 App Store Approval Improvements
This section is particularly important.

Apple's review process considers app completeness, functionality, content, privacy, and compliance with its guidelines.

Here are the areas I would prioritize.

Guideline 4.2 — Minimum Functionality
Your game should provide enough meaningful functionality to feel like a complete product.

I recommend including:

A playable campaign.
Multiple distinct defenders.
Several enemy behaviors.
Working progression.
Complete menus.
Functional sound settings.
Save and resume functionality.
Victory and defeat screens.
A tutorial.
Clear player objectives.
There is no official minimum number of stages, characters, or features required for approval.

Quality and completeness matter more than feature count.

Guideline 2.1 — App Completeness
Before submission, verify that:

Every button works.
No placeholder screens remain.
No missing images appear.
No crashes occur during gameplay.
Stages can be completed.
Progress saves correctly.
Restarting a stage works.
Audio settings persist.
The game works without an internet connection if advertised as offline.
Guideline 4.3 — Spam
Apple may reject apps that duplicate other apps or appear to be mass-produced templates.

Your game should establish its own identity through original assets, distinctive gameplay elements, coherent progression, and unique branding.

A lane-defense mechanic is not inherently a problem.

However, avoid copying recognizable artwork, characters, interface designs, or other protected elements from existing games.

Privacy Requirements
If the game works offline and does not collect personal data, keep the architecture simple.

If you introduce analytics, advertising SDKs, or online services, evaluate their data practices and update your App Store privacy disclosures accordingly.

App Store Assets
Prepare:

A professional 1024 × 1024 app icon.
Accurate gameplay screenshots.
A compelling app description.
A localized Arabic description.
A privacy policy where required.
Appropriate age-rating information.
The screenshots must accurately represent the actual game.

15. 🔥 One Special Feature I Would Add
Soul Fusion — The Game's Signature Mechanic
This is the feature I would consider making central to Soul of Dead War.

The idea is simple:

Players can fuse two compatible defenders during battle to create a more powerful guardian.

For example:

Dwarf Guardian + Bone Mage

Creates:

⚡ Thunder Guardian

A heavily armored defender that attacks enemies with an electrified hammer.

Soul Archer + Frost Witch

Creates:

❄️ Frozen Huntress

A ranged defender whose arrows slow multiple enemies.

Blood Chef + Flame Executioner

Creates:

🔥 Infernal Butcher

A powerful defender that inflicts burning area damage.

Why This Feature Matters
It creates another layer of strategy.

Players must decide whether to:

Deploy additional defenders.
Save Soul Energy.
Upgrade existing units.
Fuse defenders into stronger units.
Fusion could also produce distinctive animations and satisfying transformations.

However, I would start with only three fusion combinations.

Adding too many could make the battlefield confusing.

Fusion should also be balanced so it does not replace ordinary deployment strategy.

This could become the game's defining mechanic and help distinguish Soul of Dead War from other lane-defense games.
